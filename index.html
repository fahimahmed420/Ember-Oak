Our Process

Three slow steps to a single candle.

01

Hand-pouring

Small pours of soy & coconut wax, settled by hand at low heat so the surface stays glass-smooth.

02

Wick-setting

A single cotton wick is centred and held while the wax cools — the difference between a clean burn and a tunnel.

03

Fragrance blending

Phthalate-free oils folded in at the cure point, then rested for two weeks so the scent throws true.

Our Story · Est. 2016

It started with one bad winter and a cheap candle.

We wanted a light that felt like something — not a chemical bloom, not a jar that tunnelled after an hour. So we started pouring in a rented kitchen, testing wicks until the burn went flat and even.

Ten years on, every candle is still poured in small batches by the same small crew. We name each one after the hour it's meant for.
